**10–25 seconds:** Show database-derived review counts and the Bangla/English selector. Launch “Correction exercise: 120 → 20.” The sample exhibit and extracted fields appear side by side. Point to “Seeded fixture — not live AI.”

**25–40 seconds:** Confirm the critical fields and save. Run the deterministic checks. Show EXCESS_AREA: proposed 120 against the supplied 100-decimal holding. Open its source evidence. Explain that the engine checks the specific holding and supplied history.

**40–55 seconds:** Correct proposed area to 20, confirm and save. Show the old analysis marked stale. Switch language while editing to demonstrate retained state. Run again and show “No conflict found in supplied records,” with the scope limitation.

**55–70 seconds:** Record a reviewed decision with a short explanation. Reload the page; show the saved decision, both analysis runs and correction audit history. Ownership data did not change.

**70–80 seconds:** Open “Conflict and missing history,” confirm and analyze. Show both findings together. Incomplete evidence is never presented as clean.

**80–90 seconds:** Open the bilingual print preview and watermark. Mention that uploaded fictional PDFs/images support manual entry without credentials; optional live extraction requires explicit initiation and subsequent human confirmation.
